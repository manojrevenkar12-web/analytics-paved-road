## Time

- Periods are relative to the last complete day of data ("data through"), not to the clock.
- "Last month" is the last complete calendar month; "this month" runs from the 1st to the last complete day. Weeks start on Monday; "last week" is the last complete Monday-to-Sunday week.
- "Last N days" ends on the last complete day and includes it.
- Quarters are calendar quarters. A named month without a year is in the current year.
- Without a period, a question covers the last 30 days, or the last complete month for monthly metrics.
- Breakdowns over time: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly. Monthly metrics cannot be broken down by day or week, and their periods must be whole months (the current month counts while it is running).

## What is not answered

- Anything about individual customers (names, e-mails, IBANs, who did what): use governed SQL.
- Forecasts, and "why" questions.
- Metrics, dimensions or values not listed here.
