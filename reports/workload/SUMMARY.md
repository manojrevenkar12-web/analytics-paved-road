# Workload simulation

Generated by `apr simulate`: 300 users and automations, 30 days, 231,305 queries through the gateway and 209,584 against the baseline (the same arrivals; the gateway's extra queries are people retrying after a refusal). Access decisions, byte estimates and result rows come from the real planner and real execution on the lake (9,578 distinct queries); bytes are scaled x500 to production volume and execution and queueing times are modelled (config/workload.yaml).

The run includes the drill day's faults (config/drills.yaml): the Spark lane down for 40 minutes and a slow catalog for an hour, plus a background rate of transient engine errors in both worlds.

People retried 21,721 refused queries following the refusal message; 21,699 of the retries were answered.
Results checked for clear PII values (names, e-mails, IBANs, birth dates) for every role but compliance: 0 leaks in 9,578 distinct queries.
